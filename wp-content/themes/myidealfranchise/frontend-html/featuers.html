1-800 Radiator Is A Radiator And Air Conditioning Supplier For Vehicles. The Company Does Not Perform The Installation Or Repairs Of Its Equipment, Instead Selling These Products To Trained Professionals Who Do This Work. 1-800-Radiator Has Products From Most Of The Leading Manufacturers In The Industry, Making The Company A Popular Choice For Places That Purchase Radiator Parts For Their Own Respective Customers. 1-800-Radiator's Most Frequent Customers Are Gas Stations, Parts Store, Auto Repair Shops, Used Car Dealerships, Radiator Repair Stores, And Junk Yards. 1-800-Radiator Offers Its Customers A Lifetime Guarantee On Its Products, It Is The Top Warranty Available In The Business And One The Business' Customers Rely On.

1-800-Radiator Emphasizes Training And Assistance To Its Franchisees. A Three- Week Training Program Shows Franchisees How To Build And Grow A Business And Maintain Positive Relationships With Customers. An In-Depth Computer Training Course Will Also Show The Franchisee How To Run His Or Her Operation Efficiently. Due To Territory Exclusivity Deals Worked Out With Current Franchisees, 1-800-Radiator Has Very Limited Franchise Opportunities Currently.

1-800-Radiator Franchise Opportunities - History

In Just 25 Years, 1-800-Radiator Transformed Itself From A $3 Million Company With Two Stores To A $220 Million Company With Over 200 Franchises. In 2007, 1-800-Radiator Added Air Conditioning Units To Its Inventory, Thus Expanding The Business Even Further.

To Own A 1-800-Radiator Location, A Franchisee Must Have A Net Worth Of Half A Million Dollars, With At Least $200,000 Available In Liquid Capital. The Total Initial Investment Tends To Be About $350,000. Owners Are Also Expected To Pay An 8% Ongoing Royalty Fee, As Well As An Additional 2% Fee To Go Toward National And Local Advertising Efforts.

1-800-Radiator Business Opportunities: Other Information

The 1-800-Radiator Business Model Allows For A Minimal Number Of Employees With Less Supervision Required Than Most Franchise Structures. Due To The Preexisting Reputation Of 1-800 Radiator, Orders Should Roll In Immediately From Local Automotive Shops.
